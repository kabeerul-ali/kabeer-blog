The Universe is everything that exists, including billions of galaxies, stars, planets, moons, black holes, and vast amounts of space. Scientists believe that the universe began expanding about 13.8 billion years ago in an event known as the Big Bang. Our planet Earth is part of the Solar System, which lies within the Milky Way galaxy. The universe is enormous, mysterious, and full of wonders that scientists are still trying to understand.

Solar System

The Solar System is our cosmic home, consisting of the Sun and all the celestial objects that revolve around it. It includes eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets. The Sun is the largest object in our Solar System and provides the light and heat essential for life on Earth. The eight planets, in order from the Sun, are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Each planet has unique features, temperatures, and atmospheric conditions. Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun, while Neptune is the farthest. Earth is the only planet currently known to support life. Our Solar System formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago and is located in the Milky Way galaxy. By studying the Solar System, scientists learn more about the origin of planets, the possibility of life beyond Earth, and the mysteries of our universe.
